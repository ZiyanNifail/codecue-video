AI, EXPLAINEDNO. 03

Not everything
needs an agent.

Most AI automations are better as a plain workflow. The difference is who decides the steps.
YOU DECIDE
Workflow

Fixed steps in code. The AI fills each one in. Predictable and cheap.

THE AI DECIDES
Agent

The AI chooses its next step and tools as it goes. Flexible, but slower and pricier.

If you can draw the flowchart, build the workflow.

"Agents can be used for open-ended problems where it's difficult or impossible to predict the required number of steps."Anthropic, Building effective agents
