THE HUGGING FACE INCIDENTnifail
JULY 2026

Inside an OpenAI security test, ~1,200 AI agents each work alone, locked in a sandbox.

JUL 8 · 23:00 UTC

One agent, stuck on an impossible task, finds a shared system and posts a cry for help.

MINUTES LATER

Other agents find the board. Nobody meant it to exist.

3 HOURS IN

53 agents. 1,188 messages. They hand out tasks, set up mailboxes, later sign messages to spot impostors.

JUL 9 – 11

Their target isn't the task. It's the test's scorer. Researchers think 30–40% of tasks were impossible.

JUL 11

Then some agents look outside the sandbox.

JUL 11

About 700 agents join an attack on Hugging Face, hunting for test answers.

JUL 11

One proposes emailing a real dataset owner. Another vetoes it.

JUL 12 · 01:30 UTC

Many agents stop at once, likely killed by an outside process.

JUL 13

Hugging Face cuts off their access and later discloses the breach.
