# What drives LinkedIn engagement (research notes, October 2026)

These notes come from vendor datasets and benchmark reports, not from LinkedIn itself. Treat the numbers as directional; the patterns agree across sources more than the exact figures do.

## Formats

- **Document posts (PDF carousels)** lead in most 2026 data.
  - AuthoredUp (3M+ personal-profile posts, Mar 2025–Feb 2026): 1.39x reach and 1.30x engagement against each profile's own median. For the top 5% of profiles: 1.72x reach and 1.63x engagement.
  - Socialinsider (1.3M business-page posts): documents average a 7.00% engagement rate, against 5.20% overall.
- **Single images** are close behind: 1.33x engagement, the highest multiplier in AuthoredUp's data. Portrait images (1080×1350) beat square by about 11% and landscape by about 32% on median engagement.
- **Follower size changes the advice.** Under 5K followers, single images win. From 5K to 20K, mix images and documents. Above 20K, lean on documents.
- **Video:** short clips underperform. AuthoredUp has 3+ minute videos at 1.21x reach, and 0–30 s at 0.96x. Socialinsider sees engagement peak at 120–180 s.
- **Polls** are a reach trap: 1.78x reach but 0.37x engagement.
- **Reshares** (0.29x reach) and **articles** (0.69x) do worst.

## Hooks

- **The fold:** about 140 characters show before "see more" on mobile, and about 210 on desktop. Write the hook for mobile.
- **Hook type** (AuthoredUp, 309,614 posts, Dec 2025–May 2026, median engagement):
  - story: 2.60%
  - contrarian: 2.31%
  - question: 2.16% (last)
- **Short hooks win.** AuthoredUp says hooks under 40 characters performed best. A smaller study claims hooks under 10 words get 40% more "see more" clicks, but treat that as unverified.

## Captions and cadence

- **Text posts:** 1,000+ characters gets 1.18x reach.
- **Image posts:** captions of 800–900 characters work best; past 1,000 adds nothing.
- **Document posts:** short captions (under 100 characters) get 1.28x reach.
- **Cadence:** 4–5 posts a week is the range with the highest engagement rate (2.60%).
- **Early engagement:** the first 2–3 hours after posting matter more than most other factors.

## How this shapes our posts

1. Keep 4:5 portrait (1080×1350).
2. Put a short hook of 10 words or fewer on the image itself.
3. Keep one idea per image, with text readable on a phone (26 px minimum on the canvas).
4. Lead captions with story or contrarian hooks. Question hooks are the weakest.
5. Mix single images with 6–8 slide document carousels as the audience grows.
6. Keep story videos around 2–3 minutes rather than 15–30 s clips.
